AGCpunto25
exhibition | The Contemporary Jewelry Association | Archivio Negroni, Milan | October 16–23, 2025 and at the Biblioteca del Gabinetto di Lettura | Padua (Italy) | November 7–15, 2025

AGC Associazione Gioiello Contemporaneo is today: A nonprofit organization founded in Trieste in 2004 to promote experimental jewelry through constructive and synergistic dialogue with the national art scene and in close contact with the international community.  Now active throughout Italy, it is based between Milan and Padua and also has numerous members in other countries, including Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Austria, Belgium, Hungary, Poland, the United States, Israel, Argentina, Japan, China, and Korea. It is open to jewelry professionals, designers, students, schools, and galleries, as well as researchers, scholars, and collectors. It promotes quality and disseminates the culture of contemporary jewelry through exhibitions, seminars, research projects, and publications, fostering methodological approaches based on sharing and collaboration.

ALEGRIA JEWELS
traveling exhibition | Galerie Sophie etc...! - Paris (France) | September 13 / October 31, 2025

Alegria (Joy) explains exhibition curator Luis Acosta, in contemporary jewelry there are a variety of approaches that celebrate life, creativity, and emotional connection. It is an inexhaustible source of inspiration and creativity. Each piece of jewelry tells a personal or collective story, transforming the everyday into something extraordinary. They focus on sustainability and creativity, using recycled or unconventional materials, adding a dimension of hope and responsibility. This combination of aesthetics and social awareness reinforces the idea that art can be a vehicle for “Alegria” (Joy) and transformation. In this historical context, jewelry is not just an accessory, but a means of expressing emotions, connecting with others, and celebrating the beauty of life.




ALEGRIA / FREUDE
exhibition | Galerie Cebra - Düsseldorf (Germany) | July 16 / August 23, 2025

The journey of the traveling exhibition “Alegria” , curated by Luis Acosta, continues. This time, in the midst of summer, it is on display in Düsseldorf, Germany, at the Cebra Gallery. Stefano Fronza , along with 31 other international designers and their 166 pieces of jewelry, make up this exhibition, bringing joy, color, and vibrancy through their works—all at a time that is, to put it mildly, very difficult and complicated.

Award Ceremony - 2027

Award granted to Stefano Fronza by the Les Brucelles Association and the organizers of Brussels Jewellery Week, Brussels (Belgium)

"We congratulate Stefano Fronza for the simplicity of form at the heart of these jewels — a play of overlapping materials, immediacy, and spontaneity. The shapes in Stefano Fronza’s images evoke fleeting impressions, forming a poetic narrative that unfolds through a harmonious interplay of visual rhythm and a carefully curated color palette. At times, striking high-contrast combinations are introduced, yet they seamlessly integrate into the composition, enhancing the overall cohesion and aesthetic appeal. His work blends precision and quality with a free, creative spirit. Les Brucelles are pleased to welcome Stefano Fronza as featured artist for the 2027 edition of Brussels Jewellery Week, following this award given during Slovenian Jewelry Week 2025."

Published by Instagram profile@brusselsjewelleryweek

“A special recognition was awarded to Italian designer Stefano Fronza, who received an international prize – an invitation to exhibit at the Brussels Jewellery Week 2027. His work, presented as part of the exhibition "Veselje / Alegria" in Izola, combines photographic imagery, digital techniques, and innovative use of materials such as steel, titanium, and pearls. His In blue collection is a moving tribute to love and memory, distinguished by both its emotional depth and technical excellence.”

CATENE/CHAINS
publication | by Alba Cappellieri, Silvana Editoriale (Milan) | March 2025

Edited entirely by Professor Alba Cappellieri, this book, "CATENE/CHAINS - Storie, mode e tecniche dei gioielli più amati" , traces the history of the chain from prehistoric times to the present day. Through text and photography, the book also introduces the general public to a work by Stefano Fronza.
